# Privacy Policy – Adventure Buddies

Adventure Buddies does not collect, store, or share any personal data.

All information you enter in the app (such as adventure titles, descriptions, and completion status) is stored locally on your device using secure Android storage.

We do not:
- collect personal information
- track users
- share data with third parties
- use analytics or advertising services

Your data remains entirely on your device and is not transmitted anywhere.

If you uninstall the app, all stored data will be deleted.
